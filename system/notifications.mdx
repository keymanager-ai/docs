KryptoVault **Notifications** centralizes platform email delivery, event-based alerts, built-in system emails, and certificate expiry reminders.

It gives administrators a single place to control when emails are sent, who receives them, and which delivery provider is used.

<Info>
  Email delivery depends on the platform-wide email setting and an active delivery provider. When email is switched off, no email is sent.
</Info>

## Notification capabilities

  <Card title="Event Alert Policies" icon="bell">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Email Delivery" icon="envelope">

  </Card>

  <Card title="System Emails" icon="paper-plane">

  </Card>

## Event alert policies

Event Alert Policies connect eligible platform events with email recipients.

Events are organized into four categories:

- Authentication
- Activity
- System
- Audit

A policy can be created for an eligible event within its category and can be enabled or disabled independently.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select an eligible event">
    Choose the event that should generate an email alert.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define recipients">
    Add the email recipients who should receive the notification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the policy">
    Keep the policy enabled when the event should trigger outgoing email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send alert">
    When the event occurs, KryptoVault sends the alert if email delivery is available and the policy is enabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Shared CC and BCC addresses configured in email preferences are also applied to event alert emails.

## Policy visibility

Alert policies are grouped by event category and can be reviewed by status and event type.

Each policy retains its name, description, status, event context, and recipient list.

<Card title="Policy Status" icon="toggle-on">
  Disabling a policy stops email delivery for that event without removing the policy configuration.
</Card>

Policy creation, updates, status changes, and deletion are recorded in the audit trail.

## Email delivery

KryptoVault supports multiple email delivery providers.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="SMTP">
    Supports SMTP-based email delivery, including secure connection options. TLS and SSL cannot both be enabled at the same time.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Azure Communication Services">
    Supports outgoing email through Azure Communication Services.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="AWS SES">
    Supports outgoing email through Amazon Simple Email Service.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

One configured provider is active at a time and is used for outgoing platform email.

## Provider validation

Administrators can test a delivery provider before making it active.

A test sends a real email using the selected provider and can use either saved configuration or unsaved values being evaluated.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select provider">
    Choose the email provider to test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide sender and recipient">
    A sender address and at least one recipient are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send test email">
    KryptoVault attempts delivery using the supplied provider configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review result">
    The successful or failed test outcome is recorded in the audit trail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Active provider

Activating a provider makes it the delivery provider for outgoing platform email and switches email delivery on.

The provider must have its required connection details configured and a sender address available in email preferences.

Deactivating the active provider switches email delivery off until another provider is activated.

## Email preferences

Email preferences define the shared settings applied to outgoing mail.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sender & Subject" icon="at">
    Define the sender address and an optional subject prefix used by platform emails.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Shared Recipients" icon="users">
    Configure shared CC and BCC addresses that are included in applicable notification emails.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Email preferences also control which built-in system emails are enabled.

## Built-in system emails

KryptoVault includes standard platform emails that operate independently from event alert policies.

These include:

- Password reset
- Welcome email for new users
- Account locked
- Successful login
- MFA enabled or disabled
- Certificate renewal reminder

Each system email can be enabled or disabled independently.

<Note>
  Built-in system emails are sent only when email delivery is available and the specific system email is enabled.
</Note>

## Certificate expiry reminders

Certificate Stores can use scheduled expiry notifications to alert owners and additional recipients about certificate expiry.

A daily job checks stores with expiry notifications enabled and evaluates certificates against the reminder and follow-up days configured for that store.

By default:

- Reminder: **7 days before expiry**
- Follow-up: **30 days after expiry**

Both values are configured per Certificate Store.

## Expiry notification delivery

Matching certificates for a Certificate Store are combined into a single report.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Sends the report to active store owners and any additional configured email recipients. The certificate renewal reminder email must be enabled.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Webhook" icon="webhook">
    Posts the report to the webhook configured for the Certificate Store, with optional custom headers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Webhook delivery operates independently from the platform email switch.

A store is skipped when there are no matching certificates or no applicable recipients. If webhook delivery fails for one store, processing continues for the remaining stores.
