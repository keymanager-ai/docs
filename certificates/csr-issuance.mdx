KryptoVault Certificate Signing Request (CSR) provides a centralized capability for creating certificate requests, tracking their processing state, and maintaining continuity into the managed certificate lifecycle.

CSR activity can originate directly within Certificate Management or through **Self Service**, allowing certificate requests from different entry points to be tracked within the same CSR capability.

## CSR entry points

  <Card title="Direct CSR" icon="file-circle-plus">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Self-Service CSR" icon="user-check">

<Info>
  Direct and self-service certificate requests are brought into the same CSR management model rather than being maintained as separate request inventories.
</Info>

## CSR lifecycle

KryptoVault represents certificate requests through a defined lifecycle.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Created">
    The CSR has been created and is available within the request lifecycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validating">
    The CSR is currently in the validation stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submitted">
    The CSR has moved into the submitted stage of processing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signed">
    The CSR has reached the signed stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Imported">
    The CSR has reached the imported stage and the resulting certificate can become part of Certificate Management.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A CSR can also reach **Rejected** or **Cancelled** depending on its processing outcome.

<Note>
  The CSR lifecycle represents the state of the certificate request. Once the resulting certificate is available, the certificate itself is managed through Certificate Inventory and its own lifecycle.
</Note>

## Self Service and CSR

Self Service provides an additional entry point for certificate requests.

A request initiated through Self Service becomes part of the CSR management process so that certificate-request activity remains centralized within KryptoVault.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request initiated">
    An authorized user initiates a certificate request through Self Service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="CSR created">
    The request becomes part of CSR management.
  </Step>
  <Step title="CSR state tracked">
    The request is tracked through the same CSR states represented within Certificate Management.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This approach provides a common request lifecycle regardless of whether the CSR originates directly from Certificate Management or through Self Service.

## Certificate lifecycle continuity

CSR activity connects certificate request processing with Certificate Inventory.

When a CSR results in a certificate, the resulting certificate can become part of the centralized certificate inventory and continue through the certificate lifecycle managed by KryptoVault.

The CSR record therefore represents the **request lifecycle**, while Certificate Inventory represents the **resulting certificate lifecycle**.
