KryptoVault Certificate Management centralizes the discovery, issuance, organization, monitoring, and lifecycle management of digital certificates across enterprise environments. It gives security and infrastructure teams a single place to manage certificate operations while maintaining visibility into certificate health and risk.

## Certificate management capabilities

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Discovery" icon="radar" href="/certificates/discovery">
    Discover certificates across supported network, domain, and cloud environments and bring them under centralized management.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Inventory & Private Keys" icon="key">
    Maintain a centralized certificate inventory with lifecycle, trust, cryptographic, and private-key relationships.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Issuers & Remote CA" icon="building-columns">
    Connect certificate issuance with supported CA technologies, including ACME, Microsoft AD CS through Gateway, and Vault PKI.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Certificate Stores" icon="folder-tree" href="/certificates/certificate-stores">
    Organize and assign certificates to logical stores based on application, environment, ownership, or operational context.

  <Card title="CSR & Issuance" icon="file-signature">
    Generate certificate signing requests and issue certificates through configured issuers.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Self-Service Requests" icon="user-check">
    Enable governed certificate requests for authorized teams without exposing direct certificate-authority administration.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Operational visibility

KryptoVault provides a consolidated view of the certificate estate, helping teams monitor certificate health, issuance activity, expiration exposure, lifecycle distribution, and cryptographic risk from a central dashboard.

<Info>
  Dashboard information is intended to provide an operational and security posture view of the certificate environment rather than replace detailed certificate management workflows.
</Info>

## Lifecycle and governance

Certificate Management connects discovery, issuance, certificate stores, issuers, private keys, and self-service requests into a common lifecycle. Role-based access and auditability help organizations apply consistent governance across certificate operations.

## Why it matters

KryptoVault helps organizations reduce certificate-related outages, improve certificate visibility and ownership, standardize issuance, strengthen cryptographic governance, and prepare for certificate and algorithm migration initiatives.
