---
title: "Monitoring "
---

KryptoVault **Monitoring** provides calendar-based visibility into upcoming certificate expirations.

It helps certificate-management teams understand which certificates are approaching expiry and when action may be required, using a time-oriented view instead of relying only on inventory listings.

<Info>
  Monitoring focuses on certificate expiry visibility. Certificates are organized by their expiration date so teams can review upcoming expirations in a calendar context.
</Info>

## Expiry calendar

The Monitoring section presents certificate expiration information through a calendar-style view.

Users can select a specific date and review the certificates scheduled to expire on that day.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Calendar Visibility" icon="calendar-days">
    View certificate expirations according to their scheduled expiry dates.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Daily Certificate View" icon="calendar-check">
    Select a day to see the certificates expiring on that date.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

This gives operations and PKI teams a time-based view of certificate lifecycle risk.

## Certificate expiry monitoring

Each certificate has a defined validity period, including its **Not Before** and **Not After** dates.

KryptoVault uses certificate expiration information to place certificates within the Monitoring calendar and make upcoming expirations easier to identify.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Certificate is managed">
    The certificate becomes part of KryptoVault Certificate Management.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expiration date is available">
    KryptoVault uses the certificate's validity end date as the basis for monitoring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Certificate appears in the calendar">
    The certificate is represented on the date corresponding to its expiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review expiring certificates">
    Selecting a date displays the certificates expiring on that day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Time-based views

The Monitoring calendar supports different time perspectives so teams can review certificate expirations over broader or narrower periods.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Day">
    Review certificates expiring on a selected date.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Week">
    Review certificate expirations across a weekly period.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Year">
    Review certificate expiry distribution across a wider annual view.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

This allows teams to move between immediate expiry review and longer-term certificate planning.

## Certificate details from monitoring

Selecting a date provides basic information about the certificates expiring during that period.

The Monitoring view therefore acts as an operational entry point into certificate expiry information while the full certificate record continues to be maintained in Certificate Inventory.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Monitoring" icon="calendar">
    Shows when certificates are approaching their expiry date.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Certificate Inventory" icon="certificate">
    Maintains the full managed certificate record and certificate lifecycle information.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Operational value

Certificate expiration can create application, service, and infrastructure disruption when it is not identified in advance.

Monitoring gives teams a consolidated time-based view of certificate expirations so upcoming certificate lifecycle activity can be identified earlier.

<Card title="Expiry Awareness" icon="triangle-exclamation">
  Calendar-based monitoring helps teams identify periods with upcoming certificate expirations and prioritize certificate lifecycle activity.
</Card>
