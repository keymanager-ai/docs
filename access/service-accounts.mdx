---
title: "Service Accounts"
description: "Machine credentials and tokens for calling KryptoVault APIs."
---

KryptoVault **Service Accounts** are managed as **Service users** within Access Management.

They provide a separate user type from Interactive users and use local authentication.

<Info>
  Service-user access is controlled through the Role assigned to the account and its Active or Blocked status.
</Info>

## Service account model

Service users maintain their own identity, Role Target, Role Name, local password, tags, description, and account status.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Separate Account Type" icon="robot">
    Service users are maintained independently from Interactive users.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Role-Based Access" icon="user-shield">
    Service-user access is determined by the Role assigned to the account.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Service account creation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Service user">
    The administrator selects the Service user type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign identity">
    The Service user receives a unique username and descriptive account information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Role">
    A Role Target and Role Name are selected to define the account's authorization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure local authentication">
    A local password is assigned according to the password rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate account">
    Service accounts are Active by default and can later be changed to Blocked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account status

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Active">
    The Service account is enabled.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Blocked">
    The Service account is inactive and cannot successfully authenticate.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Relationship with Interactive users

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Service" icon="robot">
    Uses local authentication and is managed as a separate operational account type.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Interactive" icon="user">
    Represents a person using the KeyManager portal and can use local authentication or SSO when enabled.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
