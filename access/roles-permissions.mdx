KryptoVault **Roles & Permissions** defines what users are authorized to access and which actions they can perform across the platform.

Roles connect a **Role Target**, a named Role, and its Permissions into a reusable access model that can be assigned to users.

<Info>
  The current Role Targets defined in the KeyManager requirements are **Key vault**, **Certificate**, and **Admin**.
</Info>

## Role model

A Role represents a reusable permission set associated with a specific area of the platform.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Key Vault" icon="vault">
    Defines permissions associated with Key Vault and Secrets Management capabilities.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Certificate" icon="certificate">
    Defines permissions associated with Certificate Management capabilities.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Admin" icon="user-shield">
    Defines administrative access within the platform.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Permission hierarchy

Permissions are configured through module-level and downstream action selections.

Selecting or clearing a module-level permission can select or clear the related sub-module or action permissions beneath it.

Examples in the requirements use permission patterns such as:

- `Certificates:read`
- `Certificates:write`

This allows Roles to represent both broad module access and specific operational actions.

## System and custom Roles

KryptoVault distinguishes between predefined System Roles and custom Roles.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="System Roles">
    System Roles are predefined with full permissions and cannot be edited or deleted through the front-end Role management flow.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Custom Roles">
    Custom Roles are created by administrators with a selected Role Target, Role Name, status, metadata, and permission set.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Role lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Role Target">
    The Role is associated with Key vault, Certificate, or Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Role">
    A unique Role Name and optional descriptive metadata are provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Permissions">
    The permitted modules and actions are selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate Role">
    New Roles are Active by default and can later have their status updated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to users">
    The Role becomes available for user assignment within the applicable access context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigned-role controls

Role editing and deletion depend on whether the Role is already assigned to users.

<Warning>
  A Role that is assigned to a user cannot be deleted. When an assigned Role is edited, the current requirements limit editing to its Permissions.
</Warning>

This protects active user access relationships from being unintentionally removed while still allowing the effective permission set to be maintained.

## Role visibility

Role management provides visibility into Role identity, Role Target, permissions, assigned users, status, tags, and creation/update metadata.

This gives administrators a centralized view of the access models currently available across Certificate, Key Vault, and administrative capabilities.
